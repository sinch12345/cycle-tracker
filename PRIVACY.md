# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 2026



**Cycle-Tracker** is a period and cycle tracking app by **Sinchana**. We designed it so that your health information stays with you.

## The short version

- No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
- Your cycle data, logs, notes and chats with Aura are stored **only on your phone**.
- The one optional feature that uses a server is **partner sharing**. It is off unless you turn it on, and what it uploads is encrypted so that we and our hosting provider cannot read it.

## Information stored on your device

The following is saved locally on your phone and is never sent to us:

- Period dates, symptoms, flow, mood and notes you log
- Your settings (cycle length, reminder preference)
- Your conversations with Aura, our built-in assistant. Aura runs entirely on your phone. It does not use an AI service or the internet
- Your PIN, stored in your phone's secure storage. We cannot see or recover it

You can export your data, or delete everything, from Settings at any time. Uninstalling the app also removes it from your phone.

## Partner sharing (optional)

If you choose to share your cycle with a partner:

1. Your phone creates a one-time pairing code. The code contains a secret key. **The key is never sent to our servers.**
2. Your phone encrypts a summary and uploads it. Basic sharing includes your last period start date and cycle and period lengths. Detailed sharing also includes today's flow, symptoms and mood. **Notes are never shared.**
3. Your partner enters the code on their phone, which downloads the encrypted summary and decrypts it there.

What the server can see: an anonymous random ID for you and your partner, the time of the last update, and the encrypted data (which it cannot read). It cannot see your name or any health information.

The server is run by Google (Firebase Realtime Database and Firebase Authentication). Google creates an anonymous identifier for each device that uses this feature and may process technical data such as IP addresses, under its own privacy terms.

**Stopping sharing** deletes the encrypted data from the server immediately. You can create a new code at any time, which disconnects the previous partner. Anyone who has your code, such as someone who can read the message you sent it in, may be able to connect, so treat it like a password and send it only to the person you intend.

## Notifications

Period reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. No notification service receives your data.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** for period reminders, if you turn them on
- **Biometrics:** to unlock the app with your fingerprint or face, if you turn it on. Biometric data stays within your phone's operating system and is never accessible to the app

## Children

This app is not intended for children under [13 / 16, depending on your region]. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Not medical advice

Predictions and Aura's answers are general information and estimates. They are not medical advice, are not a diagnosis, and must not be used as a method of contraception. Please see a healthcare professional about any health concern.

## Your rights

Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You control your data directly in the app. For partner sharing data held on the server, use **Stop sharing**, or contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how data is handled, we will update this page and the date above. Any change that sends more data off your device will require your clear consent in the app first.
